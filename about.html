About Us

Our story, our purpose and the values we teach.

School history

Excellent Heights Academy began with a small group of pupils and a simple belief: every child can reach great heights with the right guidance. Over the years we have grown from a nursery into a full school, from creche to senior secondary.

Mission

To provide quality education that develops the mind, shapes character and prepares every student for the future.

Vision

To be a leading school known for academic excellence, strong values and responsible young leaders.

Core values

  • Excellence: we aim high in everything we do.
  • Integrity: we are honest, even when no one is watching.
  • Respect: we value every person in our school.
  • Discipline: we build habits that last a lifetime.
  • Curiosity: we ask questions and keep learning.

School philosophy

We believe education is more than passing examinations. Our teaching balances academics, technology, sports and character, so that each child grows into a confident and capable person.

Message from the principal

Welcome to Excellent Heights Academy. Our teachers work closely with parents to help every child discover their strengths. We invite you to join our family.

The Principal, Excellent Heights Academy
