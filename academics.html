Academics

From creche to senior secondary: what we teach and how.

Education levels

Nursery education

Creche and nursery pupils learn through play, songs and stories, with early reading, counting, drawing and social skills.

Primary education

Pupils build strong skills in literacy, numeracy, science and ICT, with regular assessments and parent feedback.

Junior secondary

Students follow a broad curriculum, explore new subjects and prepare for the Basic Education Certificate Examination.

Senior secondary

Students choose science, arts or commercial tracks and prepare for WAEC, NECO and university admission.

Subjects offered

Core subjects

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Basic Science and Technology
  • Social Studies
  • Civic Education

Science and technology

  • Biology
  • Chemistry
  • Physics
  • Computer Studies
  • Agricultural Science

Arts and commercial

  • Literature in English
  • Government
  • Economics
  • Accounting
  • Christian or Islamic Studies

Computer education and science

Every student has weekly computer lessons covering typing, office tools, internet safety and introductory coding. Our science and technology programme adds practical laboratory sessions and an annual science fair.

Learning resources

  • Well-equipped science laboratory
  • Computer laboratory with internet access
  • School library and reading corner
  • Sports field and indoor games area
  • Qualified, caring teachers and small class sizes
