Admissions

Everything you need to apply to Excellent Heights Academy.

Requirements

  • Child must meet the age requirement for the chosen class.
  • Pass a short entrance assessment (Primary 1 and above).
  • Present the previous school report, where applicable.
  • Parents agree to follow school rules and policies.

How to apply

  1. Read the requirements.
  2. Complete the application form on this page.
  3. Submit the required documents at the school office.
  4. Attend the entrance assessment and interview.
  5. Receive your admission letter and pay the fees.

Required documents

  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Two passport photographs
  • Previous school report card
  • Immunisation record

Application deadline: 31 January 2027
